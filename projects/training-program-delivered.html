05.3 Training Program Delivered

Cloud enablement sessions delivered with Huawei Cloud partners — data security, cost efficiency, and IaaS/ECS scalability.

Data Security & Disaster Recovery for Financial Industries

CBNCloud Connect × Huawei Cloud — Jakarta, July 2024

Topics Covered: Data security strategy and disaster preparedness for the financial industry; Huawei Cloud backup & disaster recovery solutions (Multi-AZ redundancy, cross-region replication, incremental backups up to 95% faster, Instant Restore).

Audience Size: 50 attendees — IT leaders and decision-makers from Indonesia's financial sector.

Feedback / Impact: Strengthened attendees' understanding of data breach risks and the importance of a well-tested DR strategy, aligned with public-private collaboration in building a national data security ecosystem.

Cost Efficiency with Cloud Native 2.0 & Disaster Recovery

TMS Solution Day × Huawei Cloud — October 2024

Topics Covered: Infrastructure cost optimization through Cloud Native 2.0, effective disaster recovery management, and operational risk reduction — relevant for sectors with seasonal traffic loads and legacy systems such as retail and government.

Audience Size: around 30 attendees — IT leaders and decision-makers.

Feedback / Impact: An active Q&A session on cost efficiency and infrastructure readiness for traffic surges without downtime.

Expanding Cloud Capabilities with Huawei IaaS, ECS, and Auto-Scaling

Mega Buana Teknologi (MBT) × Huawei Cloud — April 2024

Topics Covered: Huawei Cloud IaaS, ECS, and Cloud Storage solutions, with auto-scaling to handle fluctuating compute loads — complete with a live product demo, relevant for sectors with high scalability needs such as public sector and gaming.

Audience Size: around 20 attendees — general staff and mid-level IT management.

Feedback / Impact: The product demo helped attendees visualize ECS and auto-scaling implementation firsthand in high-workload scenarios.
