05.3 Training Program Delivered

Cross-industry cloud native solutioning scenarios, from retail to government.

Behind every successful cloud migration lies the same question: how can infrastructure stay resilient without straining the operating budget? From the TMS Solution Day session with Huawei Cloud to numerous other presales engagements, I've helped IT and business teams translate operational challenges — from seasonal traffic spikes to disaster-related downtime risk — into efficient, resilient cloud native architectures. Below are a few cross-industry solutioning scenarios I've designed.

Retail

Challenge: Infrastructure costs balloon during peak season, while normal traffic sits well below provisioned capacity.

Proposed Architecture: Cloud Native 2.0 with elastic auto-scaling (ECS + CCE) and active-passive cross-region disaster recovery to protect transaction data.

Outcome: Cost efficiency during off-peak periods and readiness for seasonal traffic surges without downtime.

Public Sector

Challenge: Digital public services demand high SLAs and data regulation compliance, while IT budget and internal DR capacity are limited.

Proposed Architecture: Hybrid cloud — sensitive data stays on-premise while non-sensitive workloads migrate to Huawei Cloud, with scheduled DR aligned to the agency's SLA.

Outcome: Public service continuity is maintained during disruptions, with infrastructure modernization costs kept under control.

Government

Challenge: Legacy systems are prone to single points of failure, with manual, high-risk disaster recovery processes.

Proposed Architecture: Disaster Recovery as a Service with scheduled data replication, regular failover testing, and recovery runbooks aligned to government governance.

Outcome: Measured, tested RTO/RPO, reducing the risk of data loss and downtime for critical services.

Gaming

Challenge: Server load fluctuates sharply during game launches or in-game events, where latency directly affects player retention.

Proposed Architecture: Container-based architecture (CCE) with horizontal auto-scaling and multi-region load distribution to minimize latency.

Outcome: A stable gameplay experience during player surges, with compute costs tracking actual usage patterns.

E-commerce

Challenge: Downtime during flash sales directly impacts revenue, compounded by the risk of losing transaction data without a solid DR strategy.

Proposed Architecture: Microservices architecture with auto-scaling for checkout & payment gateway, plus multi-AZ DR for the transaction database.

Outcome: The system stays resilient during traffic spikes, with much faster post-incident recovery time.
